## 1. Purpose

The Client engages MARK to examine three recently completed productions and
report where budget accuracy, production paperwork and departmental
coordination cost the Client money and time.

This is a diagnostic engagement. It produces findings and a costed
recommendation. It does not include implementation, which is scoped separately
under Stage 1 (cl. 10).

## 2. Scope

MARK will review **three (3) completed productions** nominated by the Client,
each wrapped within the preceding «12» months.

For each production MARK will analyse:

1. **Budget accuracy** — the approved budget against final actuals, by section
   and by line item. Identification of every line that varied by more than
   «10»%, and classification of each variance as estimate error, scope change,
   vendor variance or unrecorded cost.
2. **Recurring variance** — line items that were consistently mis-estimated
   across all three productions, with the direction and average magnitude of
   the error.
3. **Paperwork load** — the hours spent by the Client's production department
   producing budgets, schedules, call sheets, contracts and cost reports, and
   the fully-loaded cost of those hours.
4. **Coordination failures** — instances where information moved late,
   incompletely, or not at all between production, departments and vendors,
   and the cost consequence where one is attributable.
5. **Vendor and rate exposure** — rates paid against comparable market rates in
   the relevant territory, and concentration risk across suppliers.
